# Community & Collaboration Guidelines

Welcome to the community space for Madhu S's developer portfolio repository.

## Our Values

1. **Constructive Collaboration**: We believe in sharing knowledge openly, whether reviewing code, discussing systems architecture, or sharing insights on cybersecurity and machine learning.
2. **Respectful Communication**: Treat everyone with courtesy and empathy. Constructive critiques and suggestions are always welcomed.
3. **Ethical Principles**: Projects in this repository related to cybersecurity and ethical hacking are intended strictly for educational, defensive, and research purposes. We promote responsible disclosure and safe computing practices.

## How to Get Involved

- **Feedback & Discussions**: Feel free to open GitHub Discussions or suggest ideas for UI/UX improvements.
- **Reporting Issues**: Found a bug or responsive design flaw? Open an issue with reproduction steps and browser details.
- **Pull Requests**: Review [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for local setup, commit conventions, and guidelines.

Thank you for being part of this learning journey!
